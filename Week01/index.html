Creamy Mashed Potatoes

A super‑easy lazy‑day side dish, ready in 20 minutes.

Ingredients

400g potatoes

50g unsalted butter

100ml whole milk

salt, to taste

fresh ground black pepper

Instructions

  1. Peel potatoes and cut them into small, evenly‑sized chunks.
  2. Place potato chunks into salted boiling water, cook for 15‑20 minutes until fork‑tender.
  3. Drain the water completely while potatoes are still hot.
  4. Add butter to hot potatoes and mash thoroughly.
  5. Pour in warm milk little by little, keep mashing until smooth and creamy.
  6. Season with salt and black pepper, stir well and serve hot.
